03 — ML + DATA · BACHELOR'S THESIS

ClusterPick — predicting clustering performance with meta-learning

My bachelor's thesis at the University of Latvia, shipped as an interactive web tool: upload a CSV, and ClusterPick extracts 49 meta-features, detects dataset geometry (e.g. "Anisotropic / mixed, high noise"), ranks K-Means, DBSCAN, HDBSCAN, K-Medoids, and Agglomerative by predicted ARI, and shows a 2D PCA cluster diagram — all from a Random Forest trained on 500 synthetic datasets.

Pythonscikit-learnmeta-learning49 meta-featuresARI
AI IN THE LOOP

AI as research sparring partner: stress-tested methodology with adversarial Q&A, debugged DBSCAN edge cases, and surfaced circularity concerns in class separability evaluation before my committee raised them.

About

the artist's statement

I grew up in Hanoi, and moved to Riga in 2019 where I graduated with a Computer Science degree at the University of Latvia. My main focus is AI engineering as well as integrating AI tools to build products and services with thoughtful UX .

As an illustrator and programmer, I'm committed to creating space for both art and tech in an era when AI is reshaping both industries, using thoughtful design and technical depth to build tools that enhance rather than replace human creativity.

I speak six languages (English, Vietnamese, Latvian, German, Chinese, Spanish) and write code in one stack at a time. When I’m not shipping, I’m probably working on a new art project, exploring a new place or a new hobby.
